# SpeedTranscript

**Turn voice recordings into clear, editable transcripts with timestamps.**

[Visit SpeedTranscript](https://speedtranscript.com) · [在线使用 SpeedTranscript](https://speedtranscript.com)

## English

SpeedTranscript is an online audio transcription tool for voice memos, meetings, interviews, lectures, and podcasts. Upload a saved recording, review the transcript against the original audio, and export it for your next step.

### Features

- Upload MP3, WAV, M4A, and other common audio formats.
- Generate editable transcripts with timestamps for every segment.
- Click a timestamp to jump to the corresponding moment in the recording.
- Export TXT and SRT for free; Pro adds DOCX, VTT, JSON, and print-to-PDF.
- New accounts receive 2 free credits. No subscription or credit card required.
